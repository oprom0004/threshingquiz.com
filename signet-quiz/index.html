THE ARCHIVES OF NAVARRE

THE PRIMARY KNOWN SIGNETS

According to Professor Kaori's archives, signet abilities emerge several months after Threshing once a cadet's body adapts to dragon channeling. Here are the most formidable signets documented in modern war college records.

Lightning Wielding

Catastrophic Class

Channeled by riders bonded to Black dragons (e.g. Violet Sorrengail). Pure electrical devastation that commands the heavens and penetrates wards.

Shadow Wielding

Lethal Apex

Channeled by riders bonded to Blue dragons (e.g. Xaden Riorson). Commands living darkness, physical umbral blades, and stealth surveillance.

Inntinnsic

Forbidden Execution

Riders capable of reading thoughts and subconscious intentions. Immediately executed by Navarrian military tribunals for security preservation.

Battle Foresight

Strategic S-Rank

Channeled by tactical leaders. Grants visions of combat outcomes, probability shifts, and enemy movements before they occur.
