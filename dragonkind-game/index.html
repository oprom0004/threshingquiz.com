✦ How to Survive Threshing: 4 Golden Rules

1. Never Flinch

Core Rule

Dragons respect unyielding resolve. When facing an apex predator in the Vale, turning your back or ducking in panic will result in immediate incineration.

2. Match Temperaments

Bond Affinity

Black dragons seek unshakable moral courage. Blue dragons demand ruthless lethality. Green dragons honor tactical logic. Choose actions reflecting who you truly are.

3. Respect the Feathertails

Ancient Lore

Small or young dragons (like Andarna in her youth) are fiercely protected by the Empyrean. Defending them from ruthless cadets earns ancestral respect.

4. Expect Multiple Deaths

Patience

The Vale is dangerous by design. More than 70% of cadets perish on their first attempts. If burned, learn from the error and step forward again.
