THE EMPYREAN CODEX

DRAGON BREEDS, COLORS & TAIL TYPES

In the lore of Fourth Wing and Iron Flame, dragons are not pets; they are sentient, ruthless deities. A dragon’s color reflects their temperament, while their tail indicates their lethal combat disposition.

✦ The Six Sacred Dragon Colors

Black Dragons

Apex Legendary

The rarest, largest, and most formidable dragons in Navarre (e.g. Tairn). Known for devastating physical power and unyielding moral conviction.

Blue Dragons

Rare Sovereign

Ruthless, fiercely intelligent, and exceedingly selective (e.g. Sgaeyl). They choose riders with sharp, unapologetic lethal instincts.

Green Dragons

Calculated Vanguard

The strategists of the skies (e.g. Feirge). Green dragons value logic, tactical patience, and unshakeable discipline in battle.

Red Dragons

Fierce Destroyer

Fiery, quick-tempered, and aggressive front-line brawlers. Red dragons bond with warriors who charge headfirst into conflict.

Orange Dragons

Volatile & Proud

Unpredictable, proud, and hazardous to foolish cadets. Highly reactive dragons that demand dominant willpower to ride.

Brown Dragons

Steadfast Bastion

Calm, steady, and protective defenders. Brown dragons bond with cadets of dependable loyalty and resolute fortitude.

✦ The Lethal Tail Formations

🗡️ Daggertail

Short, razor-sharp spike forged for supersonic speed and puncturing armor.

⚔️ Swordtail

Extended serrated bone blade capable of cleaving multiple attackers in wide sweeps.

🔨 Clubtail

Solid, high-density bone mass designed to shatter shields, bones, and fortifications.

🦂 Scorpiontail

Curved piercing stinger that strikes from above, delivering fatal punctures.

⭐ Morningstartail

Extremely rare spiked mace of bone; strikes with devastating concussive and piercing force.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

THRESHING & DRAGONKIND GUIDE

What is Threshing in Fourth Wing?

Threshing is the pivotal trial where first-year cadets at the Basgiath War College enter the Vale to stand before wild dragons. Dragons choose cadets based on their inner strength and courage. Those who are deemed unworthy or act cowardly are incinerated on the spot.

How is this different from the official Dragonkind game?

The official Dragonkind website requires an email account and enforces a 4:00 wait timer every time a dragon burns you. ThreshingQuiz.com is a fast, fan-crafted tribute that requires no sign-up, has zero cooldown wait times, and provides high-resolution downloadable certificates.

Why do dragons burn cadets in Threshing?

Dragons despise hesitation, weakness, and deceit. If a cadet flinches, shows terror, or tries to approach a dragon with false pride, the dragon will execute them immediately with dragonfire. In our simulator, if you burn, you can retry instantly without penalty.

What is a Signet power and when does it appear?

A signet is a magical ability channeled through the bond between a rider and their dragon. It reflects the rider’s deepest subconscious nature and soul. Common signets include wielding lightning, manipulating shadows, foresight, and ice/fire manipulation.
